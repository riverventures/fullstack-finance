Common Objections

Addressing institutional concerns with data-driven responses

Why not a private/permissioned blockchain?
R3 is literally pivoting TO Solana. Building your own chain is like building a private highway to run a delivery fleet — expensive, isolated, and ultimately limited. Solana provides the benefits of a private chain (control, compliance) while maintaining access to $100B+ in public liquidity and composable applications.
Why not an L2?
L2s create infrastructure burden, bridge risk, liquidity fragmentation, and DoS vulnerabilities. You're building on someone else's roadmap while fragmenting liquidity away from the main ecosystem. Solana's localized fee markets provide L2-like economics without the complexity.
What about privacy?
Solana offers three layers of privacy: Confidential Transfers for encrypted on-chain balances, Contra for fully private execution, and Solana Noir for zero-knowledge proofs. This provides more privacy options than most enterprise blockchains.
Can you match competitor grants?
Solana offers the strongest organic ecosystem rather than artificial grant incentives. Short-term grant optimization vs 10-year roadmap — which serves your institutional clients better? Sustainable economics beat temporary subsidies.
How do we handle KYC?
Token Extensions embed compliance at the protocol level. Transfer Hooks enable real-time KYC/sanctions screening on every transaction. Allowlists ensure only whitelisted addresses can hold tokens. This is compliance by design, not compliance as an afterthought.
